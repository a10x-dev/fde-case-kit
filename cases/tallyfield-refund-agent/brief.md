# Tallyfield: a refund and credit agent for B2B support

**Industry:** B2B software (SaaS)

**You will speak with:** Priya Nair, Head of Customer Support

**Time:** discovery call (up to 8 questions), then 45 minutes to write

## The ask

Tallyfield sells scheduling and dispatch software to field-service businesses, from two-person plumbing shops to regional HVAC companies with hundreds of technicians. Support runs in Zendesk, the sales and customer success teams use Salesforce, and self-serve billing runs on Stripe.

Priya sent this before the call:

> "Refund and credit requests are the most annoying tickets we get. Every one needs a manager to approve it, finance takes days to process it, and customers get angry while they wait. Our refund policy is published in the help center, so the rules are clear. I want an AI agent in Zendesk that reads the request, checks the account, applies the policy, and issues the refund or credit in Stripe without a human. My team would only handle the angry escalations. We get a steady stream of these every week. How would you approach it, and how fast could we have it?"

There is also an internal admin tool for account changes. The help center policy says refunds are available within 30 days of a charge.

## Your task

Run the discovery call with Priya. Then write a proposal: what you build first, how it works, how you know it is good enough, how it goes live, and what you need from Tallyfield.
