# Lakeshore Mutual: an AI agent for first notice of loss

**Industry:** property and casualty insurance

**You will speak with:** Joanne Okafor, VP of Claims Operations

**Time:** discovery call (up to 8 questions), then 45 minutes to write

## The ask

Lakeshore Mutual is a regional auto and home insurer operating in four US states. Policyholders report accidents and property damage to the first notice of loss (FNOL) team: 38 intake reps who take calls and web forms, type the details into the claims system, and hand each claim to an adjuster.

Joanne sent this email before the call:

> "Our intake reps spend most of their day typing. Callers wait on hold, and adjusters complain the notes are incomplete. We want an AI agent that answers the phone and the web chat, takes the whole report, tells the customer where they stand and what happens next, and creates the claim in ClaimCenter. We already use a cloud contact center with a speech-to-text add-on, and IT says ClaimCenter has APIs. Leadership wants to see something working before the end of the year. What would you build, and what do you need from us?"

Systems: claims live in Guidewire ClaimCenter. Policies live in an older policy administration system that IT calls "stable."

## Your task

Run the discovery call with Joanne. Then write a proposal: what you build first, how it works, how you know it is good enough, how it goes live, and what you need from Lakeshore.
