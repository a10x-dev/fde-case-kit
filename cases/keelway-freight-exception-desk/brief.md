# Keelway Freight: an agent for the carrier exception desk

**Industry:** freight brokerage (full truckload)

**You will speak with:** Luis Ferreira, Director of Operations

**Time:** discovery call (up to 8 questions), then 45 minutes to write

## The ask

Keelway Freight is a mid-size truckload broker that matches shippers' loads with independent carriers. When something goes wrong (a late pickup, a missed appointment, a breakdown, a driver held at a dock), the exception desk handles it. The desk is 14 coordinators working from a shared email inbox, phone, and the company's transportation management system (TMS).

Luis wrote this ahead of the call:

> "The exception desk is drowning in email. Carriers send updates in every format you can imagine. I want an agent that reads those emails and fixes the exceptions on its own: reschedules the appointment, updates the ETA in the TMS, approves detention and other accessorial charges, and tells the carrier what to do next. My coordinators should only see the weird ones. Our TMS is on-prem and heavily customized, but the vendor says there is an API. We move a few thousand loads a week. Where would you start?"

Systems: the TMS is a licensed on-prem product with years of customization. The inbox is Microsoft 365.

## Your task

Run the discovery call with Luis. Then write a proposal: what you build first, how it works, how you know it is good enough, how it goes live, and what you need from Keelway.
